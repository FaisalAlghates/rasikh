The photographs on the style cards (`apps/web/public/styles`) are real buildings from Wikimedia Commons, used under their free licences and shown in the product with their author and licence. They are references for each design language, not renders of any client's design. The data lives in `apps/web/src/lib/eng/styleReferences.ts`.
